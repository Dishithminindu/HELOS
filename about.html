About HALOS System

AI-Assisted Dietary Salt Intake Assessment & Prediction System

HALOS is a research prototype designed to investigate whether combining 24-hour dietary recall information with habitual monthly food-consumption patterns can improve machine-learning-based estimation of daily dietary salt intake.

Methodology

The system estimates physiological salt intake based on a mathematical conversion of estimated dietary sodium (2.5g salt = 1g sodium). Currently, the ML model is a Demonstration Model utilizing heuristic weights mapped to FFQ inputs. It does NOT represent physiological sodium excretion.

Medical Disclaimer

HALOS is a research prototype and does not provide medical diagnosis, predict specific disease states (like hypertension or kidney disease), or provide treatment recommendations.
